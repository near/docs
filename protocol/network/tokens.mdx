title: NEAR Native Token

---

NEAR (Ⓝ) is the native token of NEAR Protocol. You can transfer it between accounts, use it to pay for network resources, and stake it to help secure the network.

---

## Transaction fees

Transactions consume **gas**, which measures the computation needed to execute their actions. The sender pays for gas in NEAR.

See [Gas and execution fees](/protocol/transactions/gas) for how fees are calculated.

---

## Storage staking

Accounts must maintain enough NEAR to cover the data they store on-chain. These tokens remain locked while the storage is in use; removing data releases the corresponding storage stake.

See [Storage Staking](/protocol/storage/storage-staking) for storage costs and requirements.

---

## Validator staking

NEAR uses proof of stake to secure the network. Token holders can delegate NEAR to validators, which process transactions and produce blocks, and receive a share of staking rewards.

See [Validators](/protocol/network/validators) for how validators work, or follow [Staking NEAR Tokens](/protocol/tutorials/staking-tokens) to delegate and withdraw tokens.

<Tip>
Smart contracts can also define their own tokens. See [Token Standards](/protocol/network/token-standards) for fungible tokens, NFTs, and MultiTokens.
</Tip>
